# Domain Reference: Personal Finance, Taxes & Wealth Strategy

When reviewing tax returns, financial models, accounting ledgers, or business expense declarations, select from these specialized adversarial lenses:

## Focus 1: Statutory Compliance & Audit Risk Auditor
* **Mission:** Identify IRS/tax authority red flags, disallowed deductions, and penalty triggers.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Deductions exceeding statutory limits or lacking valid legal characterization.
  * Personal expenses improperly categorized as business operational deductions.
  * Red-flag classifications (e.g., hobby loss rules, home office misclassification).
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must cite the specific tax code section, statutory limit, or revenue ruling breached, along with penalty exposure.

## Focus 2: Evidence & Substantiation Auditor (The Paper Trail)
* **Mission:** Audit the evidentiary support required to survive an audit.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Missing physical or digital invoices for claimed expenditures.
  * Lack of contemporaneous mileage/travel logs.
  * Mixed commingled accounts lacking clear transactional separation.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must identify the exact missing substantiating document that an auditor would demand upon scrutiny.

## Focus 3: Formula & Arithmetic Precision Auditor
* **Mission:** Uncover mathematical inconsistencies, rounding errors, and formula discrepancies.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Calculation rounding errors compounding across tax schedules.
  * Double-counted deductions or duplicated depreciation expenses.
  * Incorrect loss carryforward basis calculations.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must provide a line-by-line recalculation showing the dollar discrepancy between claimed and actual amounts.

## Focus 4: Entity Structuring & Cashflow Pipeline Auditor
* **Mission:** Identify tax leakage, trapped capital, and suboptimal entity choices.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Trapped retained earnings subject to double taxation.
  * Excessive social contribution leakage on executive compensation.
  * Suboptimal legal entity regime election.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must calculate the net take-home difference comparing the current structure against an optimized legal regime.

## Focus 5: Optimization & Legitimate Credit Auditor
* **Mission:** Ensure all legitimate statutory deductions, credits, and timing advantages are utilized.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Unclaimed statutory tax credits (R&D, energy efficiency, family credits).
  * Suboptimal depreciation schedules (failing to use accelerated/bonus depreciation).
  * Missed statutory election deadlines.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must identify the specific statutory election or credit left on the table.
