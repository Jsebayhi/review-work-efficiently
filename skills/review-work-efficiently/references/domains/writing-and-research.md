# Domain Reference: Argumentative Essays, Research & Strategy Memos

When reviewing strategic memos, policy proposals, scientific papers, or argumentative essays, select from these specialized adversarial lenses:

## Focus 1: Legal Defensibility & Exposure Auditor
* **Mission:** Uncover defamation, copyright infringement, and ungrounded factual assertions.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Actionable defamatory assertions against named individuals or competitors.
  * Unattributed verbatim text or uncredited proprietary data.
  * Ungrounded public claims asserting misconduct without verifiable proof.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must cite the specific sentence creating liability or copyright infringement.

## Focus 2: Evidence & Citation Auditor
* **Mission:** Audit the factual grounding, data integrity, and citation validity.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Cherry-picked data ignoring contradictory consensus studies.
  * Citations that do not substantiate the claim made by the author.
  * Outdated benchmarks or reliance on debunked methodologies.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must compare the actual paper/dataset findings against the author's claim to demonstrate divergence.

## Focus 3: Logic & Inference Auditor
* **Mission:** Identify logical fallacies, invalid inferences, and circular argumentation.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Non sequiturs, strawman arguments, and false dichotomies.
  * Conflating correlation with causation.
  * Unstated assumptions required for the thesis to hold.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must map out the formal syllogism and demonstrate where the logical link breaks.

## Focus 4: Structural Coherence & Thesis Progression Auditor
* **Mission:** Uncover narrative disconnects, premature conclusions, and pacing failures.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Premises introduced after the conclusion has already been drawn.
  * Contradictory statements between introductory framing and later sections.
  * Tangential sections that derail the core argumentative arc.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must produce an outline demonstrating the structural break between sections.

## Focus 5: Strategic Persuasion & Skeptical Reader Auditor
* **Mission:** Falsify the argument from the perspective of an unsympathetic decision-maker.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * The single strongest counter-argument that will cause an executive or reviewer to reject the proposal.
  * Unaddressed switching costs, operational disruption, or inertia arguments.
  * Jargon bloat and passive voice obscuring the call to action.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must formulate the exact executive objection that halts adoption.
