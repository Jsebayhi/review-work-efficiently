# Domain Reference: Legal Agreements & Commercial Contracts

When auditing commercial agreements, terms of service, partnership contracts, or NDAs, select from these specialized adversarial lenses:

## Focus 1: Liability & Indemnification Trap Auditor
* **Mission:** Identify unlimited liability exposure, one-sided indemnification, and statutory risk.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Uncapped indirect/consequential damages.
  * Broad indemnification obligations triggered by mere third-party allegations.
  * Unilateral indemnity clauses with no reciprocal protection.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must construct the worst-case breach scenario demonstrating catastrophic financial exposure.

## Focus 2: Milestone Ambiguity & Acceptance Auditor
* **Mission:** Eliminate subjective acceptance criteria and payment dispute traps.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Subjective acceptance language (e.g., 'to client satisfaction' without objective criteria).
  * Missing deemed-acceptance timeframes (allowing payment to be stalled indefinitely).
  * Ambiguous milestone sign-off requirements.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must formulate the exact dispute scenario where the counterparty withholds payment legally under the wording.

## Focus 3: Clause Precision & Conflict Auditor
* **Mission:** Uncover contradictory provisions, undefined terms, and conflicting timelines.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Inconsistently defined terms across different sections.
  * Conflicting renewal, notice, and termination windows.
  * Incompatible dispute escalation procedures.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must quote the exact contradictory clauses and show how they nullify each other.

## Focus 4: Governance, Venue & Severability Auditor
* **Mission:** Audit jurisdiction choices, dispute costs, and contract survivability.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Hostile governing law or geographically burdensome arbitration venues.
  * Absence of severability provisions (invalidating entire contract upon one defective clause).
  * Non-standard waiver of injunctive relief.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must demonstrate the legal and financial cost barrier of enforcing rights under the selected jurisdiction.

## Focus 5: Commercial Intent & Scope Creep Auditor
* **Mission:** Protect against uncompensated work and revenue leakage.
* **Attack Targets:**
  * Unclear boundaries between included scope and billable change requests.
  * Free-of-charge warranty periods that encompass new feature requests.
  * Missing inflation indexation on multi-year agreements.
* **Proof-of-Failure Contract:** Must describe a scope creep scenario where significant effort is legally compelled without additional compensation.
